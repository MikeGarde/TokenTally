Zero-Data-Collection Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 · Domain: tokentally.garde.one

The Short Version: We Collect Nothing

TokenTally does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, prompts, or device telemetry. The application operates locally on your Mac. You do not need to create an account, register, or provide any personal details to use TokenTally.

1. Privacy Nutrition Label

In accordance with Apple's App Store Privacy Guidelines, the disclosure below outlines our privacy practices across all standard Apple data categories. TokenTally does not collect any data from this application.

Data Category Status Details
Contact Info ✔ Data Not Collected No name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact details are collected.
Identifiers ✔ Data Not Collected No user IDs, device serial numbers, IDFV, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers are tracked.
Usage Data ✔ Data Not Collected No product interaction, app launches, button clicks, model views, or behavioral analytics are recorded.
User Content ✔ Data Not Collected No prompts, text, chat logs, completions, or user inputs are collected, stored, or monitored.

Browsing & Search ✔ Data Not Collected No in-app search queries, browsing history, or external web traffic are collected.
Data Used to Track You ✔ Data Not Collected TokenTally does not link your data with third-party data for targeted advertising or tracking.
  • No TokenTally Account Required: You never need to create an account, register, or share personal profile information to use TokenTally.
  • Zero Telemetry: There are no background monitoring services, event logging, or telemetry pings.
  • No Prompts or Content Monitored: TokenTally is a pricing and benchmark reference tool that never inspects or logs user prompts or outputs.

2. How the App Operates

Everything in TokenTally operates locally on your Mac. All calculations, pricing comparisons, and benchmark evaluations run directly on your device.

Model pricing specifications and benchmark Elo scores are bundled directly with the application. The app does not make network requests to retrieve this data.

Future releases may allow you to call services you already use (such as checking your balance or usage with a provider), and TokenTally will not collect any data.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We deliberately avoid third-party telemetry, tracking, and advertising services. TokenTally contains:

  • No Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel
  • No Facebook or social media SDKs
  • No advertising networks or trackers
  • No third-party crash reporters

If you download the app from the Mac App Store, Apple may collect aggregated, anonymized crash logs or download metrics in accordance with your macOS system diagnostic settings and Apple's own privacy policy. TokenTally does not combine or receive identifiable user data from Apple.

4. Website Privacy (tokentally.garde.one)

This website (tokentally.garde.one) is a static website hosted via GitHub Pages. It does not use tracking cookies, localStorage trackers, advertising beacons, or third-party behavioral analytics scripts.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because TokenTally does not collect, receive, or store any personal data or usage data, there is no personal data to retain, modify, or delete. If you uninstall TokenTally from your Mac, all local application support files and preferences are immediately removed by macOS.

6. Children's Privacy

TokenTally does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), nor from anyone else of any age.

7. Changes to this Policy

If we ever make changes to this policy, the updated version will be posted immediately at https://tokentally.garde.one/privacy with an updated revision date. Our core commitment: zero user tracking and zero data collection remains unchanging.
